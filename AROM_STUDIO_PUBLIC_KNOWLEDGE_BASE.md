## 6. Website Development Process (13 Steps)

1. **Inquiry**: Client submits inquiry form or messages via WhatsApp.
2. **Consultation**: 30-minute discovery discussion on goals and scope.
3. **Discovery**: Business requirements & target audience analysis.
4. **Questionnaire**: Interactive 16-section onboarding questionnaire.
5. **Proposal**: Itemized project specification & cost estimate.
6. **Agreement**: Digital legal agreement with milestone timelines.
7. **Planning**: Sitemap, wireframes, and database schema layout.
8. **UI/UX Design**: Figma dark glassmorphic component prototypes.
9. **Production Development**: Clean React 19/TypeScript code execution.
10. **Testing & QA**: Cross-browser, mobile responsiveness, & speed audits.
11. **Client Review**: Page-by-page client sign-off in Client Portal.
12. **Production Launch**: Domain connection, SSL setup, and live deployment.
13. **Support & Maintenance**: Kickoff of 1-Year Warranty & support coverage.
